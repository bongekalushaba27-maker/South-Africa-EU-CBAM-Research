# South-Africa-EU-CBAM-Research

# South Africa and the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)

# Research Overview

This repository contains research materials, data analysis scripts and computational modelling associated with the economic implications of the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) for South Africa.

The research investigates the implications of CBAM for South African export competitiveness, sectoral trade exposure and the country's Just Energy Transition.

# Research Objectives

1. Assess South Africa's exposure to the EU CBAM across affected sectors.
2. Examine the implications of CBAM for South African exports and trade competitiveness.
3. Analyse the potential economic effects of different carbon pricing scenarios.
4. Explore the implications for South Africa's industrial decarbonisation and Just Energy Transition.

# Methodology

The research applies Computable General Equilibrium (CGE) modelling using the GTAP-E framework to examine the economy-wide implications of alternative CBAM and domestic carbon pricing scenarios.

Additional quantitative analysis is undertaken using R.

# Repository Structure

The repository will progressively contain:

- `data/` – Research datasets and data documentation.
- `R/` – Data processing, calculations and analytical scripts.
- `GEMPACK/` – Economic modelling files.
- `results/` – Model outputs, tables and figures.
- `docs/` – Research methodology and documentation.
