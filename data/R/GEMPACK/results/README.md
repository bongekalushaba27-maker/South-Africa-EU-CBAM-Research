# Research Results

This folder contains the outputs generated from the South Africa–EU CBAM research.

## Results and Outputs

- South African export exposure to CBAM.
- Sectoral CBAM exposure analysis.
- Carbon pricing scenario comparisons.
- GTAP-E simulation results.
- Macroeconomic and trade impact assessments.
- Tables and visualisations.

## Key Economic Indicators

- Gross Domestic Product (GDP).
- Economic welfare.
- Trade balance.
- Sectoral exports.
- Carbon dioxide emissions.
- Government revenue.

All results will be documented alongside the relevant methodologies, assumptions and data sources.
